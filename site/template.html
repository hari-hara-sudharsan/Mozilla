The problem

Everyone wants to leave the frontier model. Nobody can prove it's safe.

Ten prompts prove nothing

A vibe check won't convince the person who owns the production incident. A full evaluation costs money and weeks nobody has.

"Is it good?" is the wrong question

A model can ace classification and fail extraction. One benchmark score hides exactly where it breaks on your traffic.

Models change after you ship

Providers update models silently. Without monitoring, a regression reaches customers before anyone notices.

Contributions to Otari, and what we got wrong first.

Every run is published, failures included

"100% can move" was too good

An audit found the tasks too easy and the jury favouring longer answers. We built harder tasks and a stricter jury, and published both runs.

A cut-off baseline flattered a cheap model

Grok ran out of tokens on hard math, so a cheap model looked better. Any case where the incumbent was truncated is now dropped.

Unpriced sandbox runs drained our credit

Sandbox runs report no price. Budgets now count them, and Otari enforces a separate budget for each run.
