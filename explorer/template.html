How to read this

      
    
        
  • Every line on a chart is one model's evidence, recomputed in your browser from the real scores in the run's ledger.
    • 
        
  • Above the upper dashed line, a model is proven good enough on its own. Below the lower one, it is proven worse. Final verdicts apply e-BH across every test in a run, so a line can finish just short and still be proven.
    • 
        
  • The run stopped paying for a model once its answer was settled, which is why lines end at different lengths. Stopping early never weakens the result.
    • 
        
  • dominated means a cheaper model in the same segment was already proven, so testing stopped.
    • 
        
  • The workload is synthetic, built to look like five kinds of production traffic plus two hard ones.
